JUNO STUDIO acoustic jazz sample bank v1

Recorded acoustic instruments; no original-song recordings or commercial
backing tracks. The adjacent manifest.json lists exact upstream commits,
source URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes.

1. Splendid Grand Piano
   Public-domain Steinway recordings originally released by AKAI in the
   early 2000s, according to the sample distributor's provenance statement.
   SFZ reconstruction and sample fixes: kinwie. Web conversion: smpldsnds.
   Selected PP and Mp recordings are included, without the SFZ engine.
   https://github.com/smpldsnds/sfzinstruments-splendid-grand-piano
   https://github.com/smpldsnds/sfzinstruments-splendid-grand-piano/blob/009793ebf849aee57f9a213aeaf8f2d1e2178f4f/README.md

2. Otto Rubner pizzicato double bass
   1958 Otto Rubner double bass recorded and mapped by D. Smolken.
   Copyright 2013 D. Smolken. Distributed under CC0 1.0 Universal.
   Web conversion: smpldsnds. Medium-dynamic recordings and two variations
   per sampled root are included.
   https://github.com/sfzinstruments/dsmolken.double-bass
   https://github.com/smpldsnds/sfzinstruments-dsmolken-double-bass
   License: /licenses/CC0-1.0.txt

3. Guigui acoustic drum set / Sonic Pi samples
   Acoustic ride cymbal (soft/hard), closed/pedal hi-hat, soft bass drum,
   and soft snare recorded by Freesound contributor menegass.
   Distributed as CC0 1.0 Universal. Sonic Pi trimmed these recordings;
   smpldsnds converted them to AAC/M4A and OGG/Opus.
   https://github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi/blob/main/etc/samples/README.md
   https://github.com/smpldsnds/sonic-pi-samples
   Original samples:
   https://freesound.org/people/menegass/sounds/100057/ (ride soft)
   https://freesound.org/people/menegass/sounds/100056/ (ride hard)
   https://freesound.org/people/menegass/sounds/100053/ (closed hi-hat)
   https://freesound.org/people/menegass/sounds/100054/ (pedal hi-hat)
   https://freesound.org/people/menegass/sounds/100052/ (soft bass drum)
   https://freesound.org/people/menegass/sounds/100059/ (soft snare)
   License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Distributed audio files are copied from the named converted sources.
During playback JUNO STUDIO normalizes peaks, limits long decay tails,
transposes recorded roots and applies note envelopes. No brush sweep is
included: quiet acoustic snare/hi-hat is used for ballads.
